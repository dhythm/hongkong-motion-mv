Instrumental only, no vocals. A stylish, premium, cinematic travel-film soundtrack for a 45-second Hong Kong promo video with kinetic typography. 120 BPM, D minor resolving to a bright major feel at the finale. East-meets-West fusion: guzheng plucks and glissandi, erhu lead melody, pipa tremolo, Chinese percussion (small gongs, woodblocks, temple blocks) fused with a sleek modern future-garage / neo city-pop beat: punchy tight kick, crisp claps, shuffled hi-hats, warm analog synth bass, shimmering pads and plucky synth arpeggios. Hi-fi, polished, wide stereo mix, tight and energetic, never cheesy.
